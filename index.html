What Is the IVG XL 35K?

The IVG XL 35K is a rechargeable mouth-to-lung pod system developed as a higher-capacity alternative to smaller vape devices.

Its refill configuration combines a 2ml base pod with two 10ml refill containers, creating a total stated capacity of 22ml. Rather than manually pouring e-liquid into an open tank, the refill components connect to the pod assembly and feed liquid into the vaping chamber.

The device also includes a built-in rechargeable battery and USB-C charging, making it different from conventional disposable products that are discarded after their battery or liquid is depleted.

35,000 Puff Capacity

The headline feature of the IVG XL 35K is its advertised up to 35,000 puffs.

This figure refers to the expected maximum from the full refill system under specified testing conditions. Real-world usage can differ considerably because people take different draw lengths and frequencies.

The large advertised capacity is intended to reduce the need for frequent replacement compared with smaller-capacity devices. However, the puff figure should be treated as an estimate rather than a guaranteed number of individual draws.

22ml Refill System

One of the most distinctive aspects of the XL35 is its refill arrangement.

The system consists of a 2ml base pod combined with two 10ml refill containers. Once connected, the refill containers feed liquid into the pod's vaping chamber. This design aims to make refilling more convenient while avoiding the need to repeatedly pour liquid into a conventional open tank.

When the refill assembly is empty, users need to replace it with a compatible refill rather than continuing to use an exhausted pod.

Dual Mesh Coil Technology

The IVG XL 35K uses dual 1.1Ω mesh coils. Mesh coil technology is designed to provide an even heating surface, helping produce consistent vapour and flavour.

The dual-coil configuration is part of the XL35's focus on a smooth mouth-to-lung experience. Performance can still vary according to the selected mode, refill condition, usage habits, and individual preferences.
